What Happened

An overview of the event sequence leading to the academic stall of two aspiring students.

01

Family Trust & Assurances

Two students wanted to continue higher education. Trusting an intermediary known to the family for 18 years, they were assured that credit transfers and university admission would be handled seamlessly.

02

Payments & Directive

Security deposits were paid to the intermediary. Following this, the students were explicitly directed to discontinue classes and withdraw from their current college to make way for the new enrollment portal.

03

Scam Uncovered

After multiple delays and excuses, direct inquiries with the university registrar revealed that the admission process was never completed and no application records ever existed.

Facts vs Claims

Distinguishing objectively verified and documented records from unverified personal interpretations.

Documented Facts

Financial & Registry Records Bank transactions confirm receipt of deposits, while written registrar responses confirm no application folder was ever registered.
Written Directives WhatsApp correspondence archives prove the intermediary ordered students to withdraw from active classes.

Opinions & Claims

Assumed Intentions Speculations regarding whether the intermediary planned this from the start or simply suffered operational errors.
Destination of Money Assumptions regarding exactly how the deposits were used, in the absence of verified bank trace records.

Lessons & Precautions

Essential warning checkpoints to protect other families and students.

Never Exit Enrolments Prematurely

Do not withdraw from an active college until you hold the official, validated, and university-signed admission docket for the next institution.

ഔദ്യോഗിക അഡ്മിഷൻ ലഭിക്കുന്നതിന് മുൻപായി ഒരിക്കലും പഠിച്ചുകൊണ്ടിരിക്കുന്ന കോളേജിൽ നിന്നും വിടുതൽ വാങ്ങരുത്.

Reject Verbal Promises Completely

Verbal statements and friendly relationships cannot substitute for official portal registrations. Always verify information through written reports.

വാക്കാലുള്ള വാഗ്ദാനങ്ങളെ മാത്രം ഒരിക്കലും വിശ്വസിക്കരുത്. എല്ലാ കാര്യങ്ങളും രേഖാമൂലം സ്ഥിരീകരിക്കുക.

Verify Directly & Keep Records

Contact the registrar's admissions office directly to verify apps. Demand official cashier receipts for any fee payments processed.

യൂണിവേഴ്സിറ്റിയുമായി നേരിട്ട് ബന്ധപ്പെട്ട് അഡ്മിഷൻ ഉറപ്പാക്കുക, ഒപ്പം ഔദ്യോഗിക രസീതുകൾ ആവശ്യപ്പെടുക.
